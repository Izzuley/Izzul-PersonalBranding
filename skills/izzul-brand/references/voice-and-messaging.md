# Voice and messaging guidelines · provisional

**Source status:** synthesizes owner's stated brand themes and current technical profile; recommendations below are *not a fixed finalized voice charter*.

## Voice

**Evidence-led, ambitious, technically precise, calm, accountable, approachable.** Let high personal standards show through the quality of execution; don't make client-facing copy a comparison to other people.

### Style rules

- Lead with the *client's problem, specific deliverable, evidence and next step*.
- Use readable technical language, define acronyms once where the audience needs it (SHAP, XAI, AUROC).
- Assert only what the output supports; distinguish estimates from results, feasibility from deployed success, correlation from causation.
- Prefer “developed”, “evaluated”, “validated internally”, “documented”, “interpreted” when accurate; avoid “clinically proven”, “guaranteed diagnosis”, “world's best”, or invented rate claims.
- Use active voice and practical time/scope language. Keep prose succinct without omitting important limits.
- Competitive/motivational language is an **internal compass**, not mandatory customer-facing copy.

## Audience-specific tone

| Audience | Recommended tone | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Direct consulting / Upwork | practical, solution-driven, concise | deliverables, timeframe, assumptions, tangible outcomes |
| Clinical researchers | precise, measured, transparent | cohort, design, validation, XAI caveats |
| Industry hiring | credible, technically specific | work accomplished, architecture, collaboration |
| Public personal-brand content | energetic but evidence-rich | lessons, impact, selected stories |

## Draft patterns (illustrative, NOT auto-approved biography)

**Short profile intro:** “I work on predictive machine learning and explainable AI, with particular experience applying these methods to complex clinical and research datasets.”

**Value statement:** “I help translate messy data into evaluated, interpretable models and decision-ready technical deliverables.”

**Client inquiry closing:** “If you can share your goal, available dataset and intended use, I can suggest a practical scope and deliverables.”

## Never imply

- Current MD/PhD status, healthcare professional license, commercial medical regulatory clearance or clinical-grade deployment without external proof.
- Award-winning work means every future project will succeed.
- Accepted abstracts, supplements and planned submissions are already completed, peer-reviewed full journal articles.
- An internal motto is a public brand promise until expressly approved.
- Product/business cofounder or CEO titles are relevant/accurate for every target application.

## Brand copy QA

A first-time client must immediately understand: (1) what they can hire this person for, (2) what they will receive, (3) why trust is warranted, and (4) how to engage. No positioning copy is acceptable if it obscures those four answers.
