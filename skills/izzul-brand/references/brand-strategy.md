## 3. Brand architecture and market fit

| Audience | Promise to substantiate | Right proof | Call to action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Freelance direct / Upwork | Clear ML outputs that support a specific decision | Scope, model validation, usable deliverables, relevant cases | Discuss dataset and outcome |
| Scientific / clinical researchers | Rigorous, interpretable study-specific analysis | Defined cohort, robust evaluation, XAI limitations, research context | Request analytical review |
| Employers / AI teams | End-to-end applied ML competence and project execution | Reproducibility, teamwork, shipped demos, ownership | Review technical work |
| Conferences / community | Sustained learning, innovation, leadership and impact | Selective verified recognition, real initiatives | Collaborate / invite |

**Specialization rule:** healthcare/clinical analytics is a strong differentiator, **not a constraint** that bans work in other industries.

## 4. Working client-facing descriptor (not locked)

Candidate functional descriptor: **Data Scientist & ML Engineer | Predictive Modeling, XAI & Healthcare Analytics**.

This is an *adaptation*, not necessarily the exact headline finalized in a freelance platform. Customize for audience rather than forcing the same claim everywhere. Only promise clinical validation or commercial production deployment with documented proof.

## 5. Recommended editorial structure

1. **Outcome for the reader** — a tangible decision, deliverable or improvement.
2. **How the work will be done** — data, models, evaluation, interpretability.
3. **Credibility** — one or two relevant cases, not a trophy wall.
4. **Boundaries** — assumptions, data permissions, what isn't proven.
5. **Next step** — discovery call, dataset review, scoped quotation.
